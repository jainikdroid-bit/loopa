A RedGIFs app for Android: Loopa

Loopa is a free, open-source and unofficial Android app for watching RedGIFs, and Reddit videos, in a fast, ad-free, full-screen vertical feed.

What you get

  • No ads: boosted and promoted posts are removed from the feed.
  • Instant playback: the next videos are preloaded, so they start in about half a second.
  • Search that understands you: tag suggestions, synonyms and abbreviations; sorting by relevance, top, newest, duration, likes or views; duration and sound filters.
  • Live previews in the grid, downloads to Downloads/Loopa, auto HD/SD and automatic retries on bad connections.
  • Optional sign-in on redgifs.com inside the app for "For you" and likes; Reddit subscriptions in the same app.
  • Private: no trackers, analytics or ad SDKs; open source under the MIT license.

How to install

  1. Download the APK from the GitHub releases page.
  2. Open it and allow installs from that source when Android asks.
  3. Optionally verify the SHA-256 from SHA256SUMS or the signing certificate fingerprint on the home page.

FAQ

Is there a RedGIFs app for Android?

Loopa is a free, open-source, unofficial RedGIFs app for Android 8.0+, installed from the APK on its GitHub releases page.

Why is Loopa not on Google Play?

Google Play does not allow apps built around sexually explicit content, so Loopa is distributed as a signed APK on GitHub, like many open-source clients.

Does Loopa support Reddit?

Yes. It shows videos from your Reddit subscriptions or any subreddits in the same feed and player, and saves Reddit videos with sound.
